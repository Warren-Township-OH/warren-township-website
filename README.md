# Warren Township Website

Planning and development of the new official website for **Warren Township, Trumbull County, Ohio**.

The goal is a professional, modern, accessible website that helps residents, property owners, businesses, developers, and township staff find services, public information, documents, and the correct contacts.

## Project status

Early planning. The site is under construction; this repository currently contains documentation, with no application scaffold or selected technology stack. The [existing township website](https://www.warrentwptrumbull.gov/) remains the public site and a starting source for content that must be verified before reuse.

## Approved top-level navigation

- Home
- Government
- Departments
- Zoning & Property
- Residents
- Community & Development
- News & Notices
- Contact

Subpages, final URLs, visual design, launch scope, editing workflow, and platform choices remain open for planning.

## Website and zoning portal

This repository covers the main public website, including its Zoning & Property information, approved public documents, forms, and contacts.

The separate private `warren-township-zoning-portal` repository covers future zoning, property, GIS, mapping, and QGIS work. Potential parcel lookup, interactive maps, digital zoning requests, and AI-assisted zoning tools belong to that separate planning effort. They are not prerequisites for launching the main website.

The public website may eventually link to an approved public portal experience. Its URL, integration, and release timing have not been selected. Internal township systems and nonpublic records are outside the public site's scope.
